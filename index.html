Learn how real-time graphics actually work

Free interactive units on the techniques behind games, from what a pixel shader is to ray-traced interiors. Every formula is live: move a value and watch the graph and the picture change. Every idea links to the lesson that explains it.

Small steps, linked

Chapters move one idea at a time. When a chapter uses something explained elsewhere, a link with an arrow leads there, with a preview on hover. "Builds on" and "Used in" chips connect every lesson.

Every formula is live

Coloured symbols in a formula are sliders. Click one to graph the result against it, or press Animate. The numbers are substituted step by step, so you see the arithmetic, not only the result.

Optional checks, no scores

Short questions sit between lessons, folded away until you open them. Nothing is scored or saved. The only things this site remembers, in your browser, are the theme and your engine choice.
